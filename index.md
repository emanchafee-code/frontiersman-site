# My Hunt

**Plan your hunt. Know your ground.**

A hunting map for iPhone and iPad, built Wyoming-first: parcel lines, state and federal land, hunt areas, 2026 season dates and wind on one map, with offline topo downloads and GPS track recording.

## What's included

**Free**

- USGS topo, USGS satellite imagery and Hybrid (USGS imagery with USGS roads, water and contour lines on top).
- Public and protected land in all 50 states and D.C. (USGS PAD-US), BIA tribal land and Forest Service motor-vehicle routes. Tap the map for a land card; in Wyoming that includes state trust land with its access classification and restriction notices (Office of State Lands and Investments) and BLM land.
- Parcel boundary lines in Wyoming and in the other states listed in the app (Data & sources → *Compare state coverage*). Coverage varies by state and county, and the app says where a feed is partial.
- Town and coordinate search, wind at a point (National Weather Service) and the current-hour moving wind.
- GPS track recording while the app is open, waypoints, notes, measuring and drawing, up to 25 fieldbook items, fieldbook backup, GeoJSON import and export, and opening maps you have already saved offline.

**My Hunt Pro** (optional subscription, $39.99/year after a 14-day free trial for new subscribers)

- Parcel records (parcel and account numbers, acreage, legal description and, where licensed and available, owner details) and parcel search.
- Wyoming hunt areas for elk, deer, antelope and turkey, 2026 season dates, the official regulation chapters stored in the app, Wyoming Game and Fish herd reports and selected draw-result examples.
- New offline map downloads, GPS recording with the screen locked, the 24-hour wind planner, the downwind scent cone, preferred-wind setups and wind saved with offline maps, and unlimited fieldbook items.

Saved maps and fieldbook records stay available if a subscription ends. Manage or cancel the subscription in your Apple Account settings (Settings → your name → Subscriptions).

## Support

Email **eman.chafee@gmail.com** with the word *My Hunt* in the subject. Include your iOS version and what you were doing when something went wrong.

Common questions:

- **Where is my fieldbook stored?** On your device only. Use *Create fieldbook backup* in My fieldbook and save the file to Files before deleting the app or switching phones.
- **Why does an offline area look blank when I zoom in?** Saved areas include a fixed range of zoom levels. Zoom back out, or save the area again at a closer zoom while connected.
- **Why does the map switch to topo offline?** Offline areas save USGS topo and your enabled land layers. Satellite and Hybrid need a connection and are not saved.
- **Does the app record my location in the background?** Only if you start a GPS recording and allow *Always* location (locked-screen recording is part of My Hunt Pro). Stop the recording to end it.
- **Where do season dates come from?** Wyoming Game and Fish 2026 regulations. The official Wyoming Game and Fish publications control. Outside Wyoming, the app links to each state's wildlife agency.

## Data sources

Maps and land information come directly from public services: USGS (topo, satellite imagery — USGS The National Map: Orthoimagery — and the Hybrid map's Hydrography, Transportation and Contours overlays), USGS PAD-US, BLM, BIA, U.S. Forest Service, Wyoming Office of State Lands and Investments, Wyoming Game and Fish Department, Wyoming Department of Revenue parcels, the state parcel services listed in the app, and the National Weather Service. Boundaries and ownership records may be incomplete or out of date. They are not a legal survey or permission to enter. Always confirm access with the landowner and the managing agency.
