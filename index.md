# My Hunt

**Plan your hunt. Know your ground.**

Wyoming hunting maps for your iPhone: public land, parcels, hunt areas and season dates on one map, with offline topo downloads and GPS track recording.

## Support

Email **eman.chafee@gmail.com** with the word *My Hunt* in the subject. Include your iOS version and what you were doing when something went wrong.

Common questions:

- **Where is my fieldbook stored?** On your device only. Use *Export GeoJSON* to back it up before deleting the app or switching phones.
- **Why does an offline area look blank when I zoom in?** Saved areas include a fixed range of zoom levels. Zoom back out, or save the area again at a closer zoom while connected.
- **Does the app record my location in the background?** Only if you start a GPS recording and allow *Always* location. Stop the recording to end it.
- **My Hunt Pro** is an optional subscription ($39.99/year after a 14-day free trial) that unlocks offline map downloads and background GPS recording. Manage or cancel it in your Apple ID subscription settings.

## Data sources

Maps and land information come directly from public services: USGS, BLM, Wyoming Office of State Lands and Investments, Wyoming Game and Fish Department, Wyoming Department of Revenue parcels, U.S. Forest Service and Esri World Imagery. Boundaries are approximate and are not a legal survey or permission to enter.
