# My Hunt Privacy Policy

_Effective October 22, 2026_

My Hunt is a hunting-map and field-planning app made by Emmanuel Chafee ("we"). It covers public land nationwide and has Wyoming hunting tools. This policy explains what the app does with your information. Your fieldbook and saved maps stay on your device; the app sends map and weather requests directly to the public services listed below. We do not receive those requests.

## What we do not do

- We do not require an account. There is no sign-in.
- We do not run a server that receives your location, waypoints, notes, tracks or drawings.
- We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs, and we do not sell or share personal data.

## Information stored on your device

- **Fieldbook** — waypoints, notes, measured routes, drawings and GPS tracks you create are stored only in the app's local storage on your device. Deleting the app deletes them. Use **Create fieldbook backup** and save the file to Files before changing phones or deleting the app. The app excludes its background-track file from iCloud/iTunes backups.
- **Land records** — records you view are kept in a bounded on-device cache and checked for updates when you use them online. You can clear the automatic cache in Data & sources.
- **Offline map areas** — topo tiles and land-layer copies you download are stored on your device until you remove the saved area. They do not update automatically while offline; remove and download an area again to get its current source edition.
- **Settings** — enabled layers, last map view and similar preferences.

## Location

The app asks for location permission only when you use location features:

- **While Using the App** — to show your position on the map and record a track while the app is open.
- **Always** (optional) — only if you explicitly start a GPS recording and choose to let it continue while the screen is locked or the app is in the background. Recording stops when you stop it. Location data from recording is written to your device only; it is never sent to us.

You can change or revoke location permission at any time in iOS Settings.

## Requests to public map services

To draw the map and look up land, hunting and weather information, the app sends requests directly from your device to public services: the U.S. Geological Survey (maps, imagery, protected-area records and elevation), Bureau of Land Management, Bureau of Indian Affairs, U.S. Forest Service, Wyoming Office of State Lands and Investments, Wyoming Game and Fish, Wyoming Department of Revenue, the other state parcel services listed in the app, and the National Weather Service. Requests may include the map area you view, a point you tap, owner/parcel/address/town search text, or your position when you choose a location-based feature. The app also checks our website for updated season data. These services receive your device's IP address and have their own policies. We do not operate the government services or see their requests.

## My Hunt Pro

My Hunt Pro is an optional auto-renewable subscription purchased through Apple on iPhone or iPad. Apple handles payment and billing; we do not see your payment details. Subscription status is checked on your device through StoreKit. You can manage or cancel it in your Apple Account settings.

## Children

My Hunt is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
